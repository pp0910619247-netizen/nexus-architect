Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects qualify for Nexus grants?

AI innovation, DeFi solutions, consumer products, infrastructure tools, research, and community events — all built on or integrated with the Nexus Architect ecosystem.

How much funding can I request?

Any amount subject to track guidelines. Grants are milestone-based with accountability. Final amount at the discretion of the grant committee.

Is KYC required?

Yes, KYC is mandatory for all grant recipients. KYB applies for registered businesses. We use our on-device KYC v2.0 (MRZ + liveness) — your data never leaves your device.

What chain is Nexus Architect on?

Primary deployment on Polygon Mainnet (chain ID 137). Testnet on Polygon Amoy. Plans for BSC cross-chain expansion.

Has the smart contract been audited?

Currently undergoing internal review (35 tests passing). External audit planned for Q4 2025 as a grant milestone. All contracts use Solidity 0.8.19 with overflow protection.

How does the AI Twin work?

On-device inference using Qwen2.5-1.5B via transformers.js. Quantum-inspired routing, persona distillation from twin-persona.md, and trust chain (local KB→Wikipedia→DuckDuckGo). Zero cloud dependency.
