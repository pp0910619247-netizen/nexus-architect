FAQ

How does the presale price work?

Five rounds of 100M NEX. Each round costs exactly 1.5× the previous ($0.0001 → $0.00015 → $0.000225 → $0.0003375 → $0.00050625). The contract enforces it — nobody can change it.

How do I buy?

Install MetaMask, switch to Polygon Mainnet, press Connect, enter an amount and press Buy. You pay in POL at the live Chainlink price.

How do I earn NEX32 by mining?

Use the Android app: sign in, link MetaMask, complete verification, turn on Mining Mode. Approved knowledge earns NEX32 you claim on-chain with a Merkle proof.

Is there any more supply?

No. The 1B cap is fully minted and enforced in code.

Crypto risks?

Yes — prices move, contracts are immutable. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.
