## 3. Abstract (150 words)
Nexus Architect is a live open-source protocol where every person owns a private AI Twin running entirely on their device (memory never leaves; optional browser-native LLM via WebGPU), and world problems are crowdfunded through "The Mountain" — an on-chain registry with community voting, daily quality culling, and a Human-Rights supermajority module. Rewards split 20/80 automatically via RewardSplitter: solvers earn 20%, voters/reviewers/AI-contributors/Impact-Fund share 80%. The full stack is already deployed on Polygon Amoy: ProblemRegistryV3, RewardSplitter, NexusToken (1B fixed supply), round-based Presale, JobBoard escrow (10% platform fee), and trustless TokenVesting locking founder allocation with a 12-month cliff. Working PWA + 59 passing tests + public repo make every claim independently verifiable today.
