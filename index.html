❓ FAQ
Simple questions

Short answers to things people often ask

Does it really work offline? Any internet needed? ▾

Yes, truly. The 378MB model comes with the Android app — once installed, Yuri runs on your device. No internet, no API fees, nothing to set up.

Can I use a stronger mind when I need one? ▾

Yes. You can connect an external AI with your own key, stored encrypted on your device. No middleman server.

What is the NEX32 presale? Where is it? ▾

The NEX32 presale is open on Polygon Mainnet, paid in POL at Chainlink prices. 500,000,000 NEX offered (half of the 1,000,000,000 total supply) until Oct 31, 2026. Check the live price and buy right on this page.

Do I really get tokens for mining knowledge? ▾

Yes. Your knowledge cards record a reward (difficulty 1–10 ≈ 10 to 1,000+ NEX) from the 300M Mining Pool, kept in a vault shared by the site and the app, tied to NEX32 on Polygon.

Is my data safe? ▾

Everything runs on your device. Chats and keys stay encrypted on-device, and the code is open for anyone to read (open source, MIT).

How do I reach the team? ▾

Join the Telegram group, follow on X, or visit the Facebook page — everything is open on GitHub.
